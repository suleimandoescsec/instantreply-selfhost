# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities privately to **support@instantreply.co** (subject: "instantreply-selfhost security"). Please do not open a public issue for security problems. We aim to acknowledge within 5 business days.

In scope: webhook signature/handshake handling, secret leakage in logs, suppression-list bypass, anything that makes the bot message someone it should not.

Not in scope: your Meta app configuration, your AI provider, or your hosting. Never post tokens or `.env` contents in issues.

This is a community starter with no warranty (see LICENSE). Only the latest commit on `main` is supported.
